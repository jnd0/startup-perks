Bruin is an open-source data pipeline tool (SQL + Python) for ingestion, transformation, and data quality, with a managed Bruin Cloud offering. The startup program provides $10,000 in Bruin Cloud credits valid for 12 months, a named support engineer for onboarding, a shared Slack channel with the Bruin team, and up to 25% off the annual plan for the following 12 months. Requires at least one pipeline already running on the Bruin Cloud free tier; limited to companies under 3 years old, under 20 people, and with under $5M raised from institutional investors. Smaller and earlier-stage startups can also get hands-on support to set up their data stack.
