Sourcey keeps a public registry of startup programs where every fact carries the source URL and the date it was read. That matters when you are comparing cloud and AI credits and do not want to trust a marketing page that has gone stale.

The live registry is at [sourcey.com/startup-credits](https://sourcey.com/startup-credits). It currently tracks hundreds of offers across hundreds of vendors, with machine readable JSON at sourcey.com/companies.json. Use it when you need a dated check before you apply for a credit.
