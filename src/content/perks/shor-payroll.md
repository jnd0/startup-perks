Shor is international payroll built for founders. Contractor management starts at $19 per person per month with near-instant settlement. EOR (full-time employees) starts at $299 per employee per month, varying by country. Public, transparent pricing with no demo required. Most companies can start running payroll within their first week.

Included with this deal at no extra cost:

- Enterprise support (usually $1,000+/month): a dedicated Slack channel with Shor's founders, no ticket queues or account executives.
- In-person onboarding in San Francisco or New York City.
- Lifetime price lock on your country rate.
- White-glove setup of first international hires, including country research, contract drafting, and onboarding.
- Fundraising playbook: Shor's seed deck, investor outreach templates, and a 30-minute session with the CEO before a raise.
- Free migration from Deel, Rippling, Remote, or other providers, including data export, contract transfer, and team communications.

Coverage: contractors in 150+ countries; EOR employees in 27 markets across Asia, Latin America, Africa, the Middle East, and Europe. Contractor onboarding is typically same-day; EOR onboarding generally takes 7-14 business days depending on local statutory requirements.

No equity required, no setup fee, and no annual commitment (month-to-month). Normal payroll, contractor, and EOR service fees still apply.

To redeem, [book a 30-minute intro call with a Shor founder](https://shor.cal.com/shor/shor-credit-for-startups) or [create an account directly](https://shorpay.com/?ref=startup-perks).
