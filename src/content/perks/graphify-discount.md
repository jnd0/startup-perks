Approved startups get 50% off Graphify Teams for up to 6 months. Teams includes unlimited shared repositories, uncapped graph size, 100 push builds per repo per day, 700 PR reviews per day, 800 formal verification runs per day, shared graphs on one central bill, and a dedicated Slack support channel.

Standard Teams pricing is $29 per seat per month billed monthly, or $20 per seat per month billed yearly, for the first 100 teams, then $40 and $28 respectively. Teams requires a minimum of 2 seats. Full pricing is at [graphify.com/pricing](https://graphify.com/pricing).

To apply, fill in the form at [graphify.com/startups](https://graphify.com/startups) with company name, website, work email, role, team size, funding stage, and revenue over the past 12 months. Applications are reviewed by email.

YC companies have a separate offer of 6 months of Teams free, claimed through Bookface: [graphify.com/yc](https://graphify.com/yc).

Qualified non-commercial open-source projects with MIT or Apache licenses can use Graphify free. Projects with 1,000 or more stars are auto-approved. Apply at [graphify.com/oss](https://graphify.com/oss).
